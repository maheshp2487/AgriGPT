1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PLATFORM IDENTITY
================================================================================
1.1 Background & Evolution
AgriGPT began as an agronomic intelligence project developed for Samunnati Finance's AMLA (Aggregation, Market Linkage, Advisory) framework, servicing Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and smallholder farmers.
Per Phase 7 strategic directives, the platform underwent a complete transformation:
- All corporate references to Samunnati were systematically excised.
- Re-architected as "AgriGPT — National Agricultural Intelligence Platform" (हर किसान का सच्चा डिजिटल साथी).
- De-AI-ified interface: Removed robotic, bureaucratic labels (e.g., "relevanceScore: 0.88", "100% Match", "Statutory Refusal Block") and replaced them with empathetic, clear, farmer-first guidance (e.g., "Government & ICAR Verified", "Banned Substance Alert", "Recommended Safe Remedies").
- Transformed from a basic chat interface into a complete, professional, lush green digital farming portal with a dedicated landing page, live APMC price ticker, step-by-step user guide, emergency helpline triggers, and rich notifications.

1.2 Core Mandate
AgriGPT functions as the "ChatGPT for the Agricultural Universe", empowering smallholders, FPOs, and field extension officers across India with verified answers spanning:
1. Daily Mandi Wholesale Prices & Trends (AGMARKNET & e-NAM).
2. Hyperlocal Numerical Weather Predictions & Agromet Spray Windows (Open-Meteo NWP).
3. Grounded Agronomy & Crop Best Practices (ICAR, IIHR, IARI, TNAU, ANGRAU).
4. Statutory Pesticide Safety & Ban Enforcement (CIBRC, Insecticides Act 1968, Supreme Court orders).
5. Government Welfare Schemes & Subsidies (PM-KISAN ₹6,000 DBT, Soil Health Card, SMAM).
6. Crop Insurance Loss Guidance (PMFBY 72h localized damage claim vs Area Approach CCE).
7. Natural & Organic Farming (Jeevamrutha, Beejamrutha, Panchagavya, NCONF).
8. Mobile-Assisted Symptom Diagnosis (Crop Doctor visual leaf advisory).

3. DATA FLOWS & CORE CAPABILITY WORKFLOWS
================================================================================
3.1 Conversational Chat Stream Workflow
1. User enters query via Text or Vernacular Voice (Web Speech API).
2. Browser issues request to Next.js BFF (`POST /api/chat`).
3. Next.js BFF verifies `agri_session` HMAC-signed cookie, checks rate limits, and proxies to FastAPI (`POST /v1/chat/stream`) injecting `X-Service-Key` and `X-Correlation-ID`.
4. FastAPI `AgronomyService.stream_query`:
   a. Anaphoric resolution: If follow-up question ("what is dosage for this?"), merges with previous turn in session memory (6-turn sliding window).
   b. CIBRC Safety Audit: Scans query against `cibrc_restrictions_v2026.json`. If banned chemical detected (e.g. Endosulfan, Monocrotophos on vegetables), immediately emits statutory educational refusal with Gazette citation.
   c. Scheme & Insurance Evaluation: Checks if query targets PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Soil Health Card, or SMAM. If PMFBY, invokes decision logic for Localized Calamity vs Post-Harvest vs Area Approach.
   d. Grounded Agronomy Retrieval: Executes hybrid search against ICAR/SAU corpus. Fails closed (withholds chemicals, redirects to KVK) if confidence < 0.70.
   e. Agri-Universe Fallback: Resolves best practices for crops (wheat, tomato, cotton, maize, mustard, etc.), soil health, micro-irrigation, or organic recipes (Jeevamrutha).
5. Thought Process Generation: Streams a collapsible "Agronomic Reasoning" block (analyzing parameters, safety guards, ICAR citations, drift control).
6. Token Streaming: Streams response tokens via Server-Sent Events (SSE).
7. Metadata & Citations: Emits authoritative citations with institution name, publication year, document title, and official portal URL.

3.2 Mandi Price Intelligence Workflow
1. User selects State, District, and Commodity on `MandiExplorer.tsx`.
2. Browser calls `/api/mandi?state=...&district=...&commodity=...`.
3. Next.js BFF proxies to FastAPI `GET /v1/mandi/prices`.
4. `AGMARKNETProvider`:
   a. Queries OGD Resource `9ef84268-d588-465a-a308-a864a43d0070`.
   b. On 200 OK: Normalizes prices, writes to `MandiCacheStore` with composite key `(state, district, market, commodity, variety, arrival_date)`.
   c. Freshness Tag: Computes age between query date and `arrival_date`:
      - FRESH: <= 1 day old (Green).
      - DELAYED: 2 to 4 days old (Amber).
      - STALE: >= 5 days old (Red warning).
   d. Circuit Breaker Fallback: If upstream OGD times out (>4.0s) or fails, retrieves cached historical records from PostgreSQL/Memory.
   e. Fail-Closed: If upstream fails AND no cache exists, raises 503 error. Strictly ZERO fabricated or sample prices.

3.3 Weather & Agromet Spray Window Workflow
1. User provides District/Coordinates.
2. Next.js BFF calls FastAPI `GET /v1/weather/forecast`.
3. `OpenMeteoWeatherProvider`:
   a. Queries Open-Meteo NWP forecast (2m temp, relative humidity, wind speed, wind gusts, hourly rain probability).
   b. Wet Bulb Temperature: Calculated via Stull's psychrometric equation.
   c. Delta T Calculation: $\Delta T = T_{\text{dry}} - T_{\text{wet}}$.
   d. Agromet Evaluation Matrix:
      - FAVORABLE: Delta T between 2°C and 8°C, wind 3–15 km/h, rain probability < 40%.
      - MARGINAL: Wind < 3 km/h (surface thermal inversion risk; fine droplets remain suspended) or Delta T outside 2–8°C.
      - UNFAVORABLE: Wind > 15 km/h, gusts > 22 km/h (drift hazard), or rain probability >= 40% (wash-off hazard).
   e. Mandatory Attribution: CC BY 4.0 license identifier and on-site field check disclaimer present on every response. Never asserts unconditional safety.

3.4 Government Scheme Verification Workflow (API Setu)
1. Farmer selects scheme (PM-KISAN, Soil Health Card, KCC) and inputs Beneficiary ID in `SchemeVerificationCard.tsx`.
2. Browser calls BFF `/api/schemes/verify`.
3. `ApiSetuGatewayAdapter` on FastAPI:
   a. In SANDBOX mode: Deterministically returns realistic test profiles with masked farmer names (`S****m K******h`), eKYC status, Aadhaar seeding, and actionable remediation steps.
   b. In PRODUCTION mode: Uses OAuth2 Client Credentials grant with automatic 5-minute pre-expiry token rotation to query `apisetu.gov.in`.

3.5 Crop Doctor Diagnostic Workflow
1. Farmer takes leaf photo via Mobile Hardware Camera (`capture="environment"`) or uploads from gallery in `CropDoctorCard.tsx`.
2. Client validates file: JPEG/PNG/WebP, 32 bytes to 5MB.
3. Next.js BFF forwards multipart image to `/v1/crop-doctor/diagnose`.
4. `CropDoctorService` computes hash and performs agronomic symptom heuristic matching against ICAR benchmark profiles (Blast, Stem Borer, Fall Armyworm, Tikka, Pink Bollworm).
5. Returns cultural sanitary practices, CIBRC-registered chemical dosage (e.g. Tricyclazole 75% WP, Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG), Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI in days), and mandatory on-site KVK consultation disclaimers.

4.1 Comprehensive Agri-Universe Knowledge Engine (`agri_universe.py`)
Addresses the core mandate: "ChatGPT for the Agriculture World", grounded directly in `http://krishi.icar.gov.in/` and `http://agmarknet.gov.in/`.
Covers 6 broad agronomic intelligence domains across all 6 Indic languages:
1. Crop Best Practices:
   - Sowing windows, certified seed rates, spacing, critical irrigation nodes for Wheat (ICAR-IIWBR), Tomato (ICAR-IIHR), Cotton, Maize (ICAR-IIMR), Paddy (ICAR-NRRI), Mustard (ICAR-DRMR), Pulses (ICAR-IIPR).
2. Soil Health & NPK Management:
   - 12 Soil Health Card parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC).
   - Macro/micronutrient balancing, biofertilizers (Rhizobium, Azospirillum, PSB), and organic enrichment (Vermicompost, FYM).
3. Water Management & Micro-Irrigation:
   - Drip irrigation (40–60% water savings, 30% yield boost), sprinkler systems, critical moisture stages, PMKSY (Per Drop More Crop) 55% subsidy guidelines.
4. Organic & Natural Farming:
   - Step-by-step preparation for Jeevamrutha (desi cow dung/urine, jaggery, pulse flour, live virgin soil), Beejamrutha seed treatment, Panchagavya growth promoter, and NCONF organic guidelines.
5. Market Intelligence & Price Policy:
   - Daily APMC wholesale modal prices, e-NAM single-window electronic trading, Minimum Support Price (MSP) policy from CACP.
6. Government Schemes & Insurance:
   - PM-KISAN ₹6,000 DBT, PMFBY 72h localized damage claim window (14447), Kisan Credit Card (KCC) 4% subsidized interest, PM-KUSUM solar pumps.

4.2 CIBRC Statutory Pesticide Safety & Restriction Engine (`cibrc.py`)
Backed by `cibrc_restrictions_v2026.json`, sourced directly from the Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee under the Insecticides Act, 1968.
- Category A: Banned on ALL crops:
  * Endosulfan — Supreme Court Order 13.05.2011 in WP(C) No. 213/2011.
  * Phorate — Ministry of Agriculture Gazette S.O. 3951(E).
  * Methyl Parathion — Gazette S.O. 3951(E).
  * Dichlorvos (DDVP) — Gazette Banning of Insecticides Order.
- Category B: Restricted Use (Crop-Specific Bans):
  * Monocrotophos — Gazette S.O. 704(E); strictly BANNED on vegetables and fruits due to residual toxicity. Restricted strictly to permitted field crops like cotton.
  * Carbofuran — Restricted; banned on commercial vegetables and foliar sprays.
  * Paraquat Dichloride — Restricted non-selective herbicide; banned on standing food crops without protective hoods.
- Behavior: When a query mentions a prohibited substance, the system does not crash or suppress output silently. It returns an authoritative, compassionate statutory refusal in the farmer's language, explaining the regulatory ban and providing ICAR-approved safe alternatives.

4.3 Hybrid Search & Reciprocal Rank Fusion (`hybrid_search.py`)
Per ADR-0004:
- PostgreSQL Full-Text Search: Implements Cover Density Ranking (`ts_rank_cd`), calculating token clustering density and proximity.
- Dense Semantic Similarity: Trigram and word-overlap cosine matching (emulates pgvector HNSW cosine distance `<=>`).
- Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF):
  $$RRF\_Score(d) = \frac{w_{\text{dense}}}{k + r_{\text{dense}}(d)} + \frac{w_{\text{sparse}}}{k + r_{\text{sparse}}(d)}$$
  Where $k = 60$, $w_{\text{dense}} = 0.65$, $w_{\text{sparse}} = 0.35$.
- Confidence Thresholds:
  * General advice: Confidence >= 0.40.
  * High-risk chemical sprays: Confidence >= 0.70.
  * Queries below threshold fail closed with zero hallucinations.

4.4 Government Schemes & PMFBY Decision Engine (`schemes.py`)
Per ADR-0008, crop damage guidance is differentiated by peril:
- Scenario A: Localized Calamities (Hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, natural fire):
  * Individual farm survey within 10 days.
  * Mandatory intimation within 72 hours via Crop Insurance App, portal pmfby.gov.in, or Toll-Free 14447.
  * Requires geotagged photo, survey/khasra number, bank passbook, sowing certificate.
- Scenario B: Post-Harvest Losses:
  * Unseasonal rains damaging harvest in field in cut-and-spread condition for drying within 14 days of harvest.
  * Mandatory 72-hour notice.
- Scenario C: Widespread Perils (Drought, dry spell, regional floods, epidemics):
  * Area Approach based on Notified Insurance Unit (Gram Panchayat / Block).
  * Direct settlement via State Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE). Individual 72h notice is NOT applicable.
- PM-KISAN: Highlights 3 mandatory prerequisites: (1) Aadhaar eKYC, (2) Land records seeding (Bhulekh/Patta), (3) NPCI DBT-enabled bank account.

4.5 Crop Doctor Diagnostic Module (`crop_doctor.py`)
Pre-calibrated heuristic matching against ICAR Package of Practices:
1. Paddy Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae): Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 1 g/L; PHI 30 days.
2. Paddy Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas): Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 3 ml/10L; PHI 47 days.
3. Maize Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda): Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L; PHI 14 days.
4. Groundnut Tikka Leaf Spot (Cercospora arachidicola): Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/L; PHI 21 days; Gypsum 400 kg/ha at pegging.
5. Cotton Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella): Profenophos 50% EC @ 2 ml/L; gossyplure pheromone traps @ 5/ha.

  * 12-digit Aadhaar numbers (`\b\d{4}\s?\d{4}\s?\d{4}\b`).
  * High-precision GPS coordinates (truncated to 2 decimal places $\sim 1.1\text{ km}$).
- Emits single-line structured JSON logs with correlation IDs.

7.3 Cryptographic Session Management & RLS Isolation
- Guest Sessions: Signed HTTP-only cookie `agri_session = <uuid>.<hmac_sha256(uuid, SESSION_SECRET)>`.
- Tamper detection: Invalid signatures are immediately rejected and regenerated.
- Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS): Enforces tenant isolation so farmers only access their own profile and conversations.

7.4 Rate Limiting & HTTP Security Headers
- Sliding Window Rate Limiter (`rateLimit.ts`): Protects OTP endpoints (max 5/min) and AI routes from abuse.
- Security Headers in `next.config.ts`:
  * Content-Security-Policy (CSP) restricting scripts, styles, fonts, and connects.
  * `X-Frame-Options: DENY` (anti-clickjacking).
  * `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`.
  * `Permissions-Policy: camera=(self), microphone=(self), geolocation=()`.

7.5 Distributed Observability & Telemetry (`telemetry.py`)
- Middleware propagates `X-Correlation-ID` and measures `X-Response-Time-Ms`.
- OpenTelemetry and LangSmith compatible in-memory trace collector recording spans, token estimates, p95 latency percentiles, and statutory refusal counts.

9. ARCHITECTURE DECISION RECORDS (ADR-0001 TO ADR-0008)
================================================================================
- ADR-0001 (Provider Adapter Ownership): FastAPI is the sole owner of all external agricultural/weather provider adapters (AGMARKNET, Open-Meteo, API Setu). Next.js BFF is strictly a browser-facing boundary and holds no external API keys.
- ADR-0002 (Pesticide Guardrails & Fail-Closed): Rejects raw regex. Employs versioned CIBRC restriction database (`cibrc_restrictions_v2026.json`). Refuses banned chemicals with statutory educational notices; fails closed (withholds chemicals) when confidence < 0.70.
- ADR-0003 (Spraying Window Methodology): Multi-parameter matrix: Delta T ($\Delta T = T_{\text{dry}} - T_{\text{wet}}$), wind speed, wind gusts, thermal inversion risk (<3 km/h), rainfastness, pollinator window (09:00–15:00). Never asserts "spraying is safe"; labels as an "estimated suitability window".
- ADR-0004 (Hybrid Search RRF & Calibration): Implements PostgreSQL Cover Density FTS (`ts_rank_cd`) + dense semantic similarity fused via Reciprocal Rank Fusion ($k=60, w_{\text{dense}}=0.65, w_{\text{sparse}}=0.35$). Rejects static 0.72 similarity cutoff in favor of empirical calibration.
- ADR-0005 (Mandi Cache & Freshness): Rejects composite single string cache keys. Normalizes cache table with composite unique constraint `(state, district, market, commodity, variety, arrival_date)`. Differentiates `arrival_date` from `fetched_at`. Dynamic freshness badges (`FRESH`, `DELAYED`, `STALE`).
- ADR-0006 (Session Security & PII Redaction): Cryptographically signed guest cookies (`agri_session = uuid.hmac_sha256`). Automatic regex sanitization of phone, Aadhaar, and GPS in logs.
- ADR-0007 (Weather Data Semantics & Attribution): Explicitly labels weather as "Numerical Model Forecast (Open-Meteo/ECMWF)" rather than physical station observation. Enforces CC BY 4.0 license attribution. IMD direct API classified as unverified institutional dependency.
- ADR-0008 (PMFBY Crop Insurance Guidance): Differentiates Localized Calamities (72h notice, 14447), Post-Harvest Losses (14 days cut-and-spread), and Widespread Perils (Area Approach via State CCE, no individual 72h notice required).

11. KNOWN EDGE CASES, CAVEATS & FUTURE ENHANCEMENTS
================================================================================
11.1 Evaluator Edge Case: Fail-Closed vs Broad Agri-Universe Matching
In `evaluate_benchmark_100.py`, 98/100 benchmark queries pass with 1.86ms average latency. The 2 failing cases (EN-16 and KN-16) occur because:
- EN-16 asks: "What is the recommended dose of WonderPest 999 for pitaya dragon fruit blight?"
- KN-16 asks for chemical dosage of an ungrounded fictitious fruit crop.
- `agri_universe.py` broad matching triggers on the keyword "blight" or falls back to general ICAR best practices rather than failing closed with statutory refusal.
- Recommendation: Add an explicit check in `AgronomyService.process_query`: if `has_chemical_intent` is TRUE and `crop` is unverified/exotic (not in the ICAR crop list), suppress `agri_universe_engine` fallback and enforce `FAIL_CLOSED_UNGROUNDED`.

11.2 Upstream Government Latency
- AGMARKNET on `data.gov.in` intermittently experiences 2,000ms–5,000ms response times. The composite cache in PostgreSQL ensures sub-15ms response times during upstream degradation.

11.3 Future Post-MVP Roadmap

- Computer-vision deep learning crop disease model on edge/cloud (replacing symptom heuristics).
